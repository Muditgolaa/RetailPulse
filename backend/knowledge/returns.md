# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window

Most products bought from NovaMart, online or in a store, can be returned within 10 days of delivery or store pickup.

Gold members get a 15-day return window and Platinum members get a 30-day return window, as described in the Membership Benefits policy.

Opened mobile phones, tablets, laptops, TVs and audio products follow the Opened Electronics Policy, and their return windows are not extended by membership.

## Eligibility for a return

A product is eligible for return when all of the following are true:

- The return is requested within the return window.
- The product is unused and in the same condition it was received in.
- The original packaging, tags, manuals, accessories and free gifts are included.
- The invoice or order number is provided.

## Products that cannot be returned

The following products cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective:

- Gift cards and NovaMart vouchers.
- Personal care and grooming appliances once the seal is opened.
- Software, game codes and digital subscriptions once activated.
- Products marked "Final Sale" on the product page or receipt.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items

If a product arrives damaged, is defective, or is not the item ordered, report it within 48 hours of delivery through Support or at any NovaMart store.

Include photos of the product and packaging. After verification, NovaMart offers a replacement, or a full refund if a replacement is not in stock.

NovaMart arranges a free pickup for damaged, defective or wrong items. These returns are accepted even for products that normally cannot be returned.

## Discounted and sale items

Discounted products can be returned within the normal return window, unless they are marked "Final Sale".

The refund is the amount actually paid, after discounts and coupons.

Discounted products bought online can be returned at any NovaMart store or through a doorstep pickup.

## How to return a product

Online orders can be returned through a doorstep pickup booked from the order page or with Support, or at any NovaMart store.

In-store purchases can be returned at any NovaMart store with the invoice.

Doorstep pickup is free for eligible returns.

## Refund timelines

NovaMart inspects a returned product within 2 business days of receiving it. After approval, the refund is issued to the original payment method:

- UPI and wallets: 1 to 3 business days.
- Net banking: 3 to 5 business days.
- Credit and debit cards: 5 to 7 business days.
- Cash on delivery orders: refunded by bank transfer (NEFT) within 5 to 7 business days after the customer shares bank details.

Customers can choose NovaMart store credit instead. Store credit is added within 2 hours of approval.

## Cancellations

An order can be cancelled free of charge before it is shipped or before it is ready for store pickup.

An order that is delayed beyond its expected delivery date can be cancelled for a full refund, even after it is shipped.
