# Membership Benefits

<!-- Sections marked with a "tier" comment apply only to that tier. Ingestion can copy it into the chunk metadata. -->

## Membership tiers and how to qualify

NovaMart has four membership tiers, based on the amount spent in the last 12 months:

- Standard: free to join.
- Silver: ₹25,000 or more.
- Gold: ₹75,000 or more.
- Platinum: ₹2,00,000 or more.

Tiers are reviewed every year on the membership anniversary date.

## Standard member benefits
<!-- tier: STANDARD -->

- Earn 1 NovaPoint for every ₹100 spent.
- Free standard delivery on orders of ₹999 or more.
- 10-day return window, as described in the Returns and Refunds Policy.
- Express delivery costs ₹149 per order in Delhi NCR.

## Silver member benefits
<!-- tier: SILVER -->

- Earn 2 NovaPoints for every ₹100 spent.
- Free standard delivery on all orders, with no minimum order value.
- 10-day return window.
- Express delivery costs ₹149 per order in Delhi NCR.

## Gold member benefits
<!-- tier: GOLD -->

- Earn 3 NovaPoints for every ₹100 spent.
- Free standard delivery on all orders, with no minimum order value.
- Free express delivery on orders of ₹2,000 or more in Delhi NCR, where express delivery is available. Below ₹2,000, express delivery costs ₹149.
- Extended return window of 15 days for products covered by the standard Returns and Refunds Policy.
- Priority customer support.

## Platinum member benefits
<!-- tier: PLATINUM -->

- Earn 5 NovaPoints for every ₹100 spent.
- Free standard delivery on all orders.
- Free express delivery on all orders in Delhi NCR, with no minimum order value.
- Extended return window of 30 days for products covered by the standard Returns and Refunds Policy.
- A free 1-year NovaCare extended warranty on large appliances, as described in the Warranty Policy.
- A dedicated support phone line.

## What extended return windows do not cover

The longer return windows for Gold and Platinum members do not apply to opened electronics. Opened mobile phones, tablets, laptops, TVs, headphones, earbuds and wearables always follow the Opened Electronics Policy.

## NovaPoints

- Each NovaPoint is worth ₹0.25 when redeemed at checkout.
- NovaPoints expire 12 months after they are earned.
- Points are not earned on gift card purchases.
- Points used on an order that is returned are added back to the account.
