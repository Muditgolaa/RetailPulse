# Payment Methods Policy

## Accepted payment methods online

NovaMart accepts these payment methods for online orders:

- UPI.
- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay and American Express.
- Net banking from major Indian banks.
- NovaMart gift cards.
- NovaPoints, as described in the Membership Benefits policy.
- Cash on delivery, subject to the limits below.

## Accepted payment methods in stores

NovaMart stores accept cash, credit and debit cards, UPI and NovaMart gift cards.

## Cash on delivery

- Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹50,000.
- It is available only for pincodes where the checkout page shows it.
- It is not available for large appliances or for express delivery orders.
- Customers can pay the delivery agent in cash or by UPI.

## EMI options

No-cost and standard EMI are available on credit cards from partner banks for orders of ₹10,000 or more. The checkout page shows the EMI plans available for each card.

## Split payments

An order can be paid with a NovaMart gift card or NovaPoints together with one other payment method.

## Failed or duplicate payments

If money is debited but the order is not confirmed, the amount is reversed to the original payment method automatically within 5 to 7 business days.

## Payment security

NovaMart never asks for a card PIN, CVV or OTP over the phone, chat or email. Card details are processed by a certified payment gateway and are not stored by NovaMart.
