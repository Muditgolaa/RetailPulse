# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery options

NovaMart offers three ways to receive an order:

- **Standard delivery** to serviceable pincodes across India.
- **Express delivery**, which is same-day or next-day, in Delhi NCR only.
- **Store pickup** (click and collect) at any NovaMart store.

## Where delivery is available

Delivery eligibility depends on the delivery pincode.

- Standard delivery is available to all serviceable pincodes in India. The checkout page shows whether a pincode is serviceable.
- Express delivery is available only in Delhi NCR: Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad.
- Large appliances, such as refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners and TVs of 43 inches or more, are delivered only within Delhi NCR and to the metro cities of Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Pune.
- Cash on delivery is not available for every pincode. See the Payment Methods Policy.

## Delivery timelines

Standard delivery timelines after the order is shipped:

- Delhi NCR: 2 to 3 business days.
- Other metro cities: 3 to 5 business days.
- Rest of India: 5 to 8 business days.

Express delivery: orders placed before 2 pm are delivered the same day, and orders placed after 2 pm are delivered the next day. Express delivery depends on stock at a nearby Delhi NCR store.

## Delivery fees

- Standard delivery is free for orders of ₹999 or more. Below ₹999, the fee is ₹49.
- Express delivery costs ₹149 per order.
- Large appliance delivery and installation scheduling are free within the areas where large appliances are delivered.
- Some membership tiers get free standard or express delivery. See the Membership Benefits policy.

## Store pickup

A store pickup order is ready within 2 hours when the products are in stock at the chosen store. The customer receives a message when it is ready.

Orders are held at the store for 3 days. Store pickup is free. Bring the order number and a photo ID.

## Large appliance installation

Installation of large appliances is scheduled within 48 hours of delivery by NovaMart or the brand's technician. Installation is free for refrigerators, washing machines and TVs. Air conditioner installation is charged as shown on the product page.

## Delayed orders

If an order passes its expected delivery date, NovaMart notifies the customer with a new estimated date.

The customer can cancel a delayed order that has not been delivered for a full refund, as stated in the Returns and Refunds Policy.
