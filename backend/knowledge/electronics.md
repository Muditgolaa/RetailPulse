# Opened Electronics Policy

## Scope

This policy covers electronics whose box seal has been broken: mobile phones, tablets, laptops, TVs, headphones, earbuds, speakers and smartwatches.

Sealed, unopened electronics follow the standard Returns and Refunds Policy.

The windows in this policy apply to all membership tiers and are not extended by Gold or Platinum membership.

## Opened mobile phones and tablets

An opened mobile phone or tablet can be returned within 7 days of delivery only if it is defective or not as described. A change of mind is not accepted once the seal is broken.

The defect must be confirmed by a NovaMart store technician or by the brand's authorised service centre.

Before the return, the customer must remove screen locks and accounts such as Google or Apple ID, and reset the device to factory settings.

## Opened laptops

An opened laptop can be returned within 7 days of delivery.

- If it is defective, the return is free.
- For a change of mind, a 10% restocking fee is deducted from the refund. The laptop must have no physical damage and must include the box, charger and all accessories.

## Opened TVs and large screens

An opened TV can be returned or replaced within 10 days of installation only if a defect is confirmed by the brand's technician. A change of mind is not accepted after installation.

## Opened headphones, earbuds and wearables

For hygiene reasons, opened headphones, earbuds and smartwatches can be returned within 7 days of delivery only if they are defective.

## Replacement before refund

For a confirmed defect, NovaMart first offers a replacement of the same model. If the same model is out of stock at the customer's store and warehouse, a full refund is issued instead.

## Customer data

Customers are responsible for backing up and erasing their personal data before handing over any device. NovaMart is not responsible for data left on returned devices.
