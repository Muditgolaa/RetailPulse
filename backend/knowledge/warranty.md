# Warranty Policy

## Warranty periods by product category

Products sold by NovaMart carry the manufacturer's warranty from the date on the invoice. The warranty period depends on the product category:

- Mobile phones and tablets: 1 year.
- Laptops: 1 year, unless the product page states a longer brand warranty.
- TVs: 1 year.
- Refrigerators: 1 year on the product. The compressor is covered for the longer period stated on the product page, up to 10 years.
- Washing machines: 2 years on the product. The motor is covered for the period stated on the product page.
- Air conditioners: 1 year on the product and 5 years on the compressor.
- Headphones, speakers and other audio products: 1 year.
- Chargers, cables, cases and other small accessories: 6 months.
- NovaTech (NovaMart's own brand) products: 2 years on all NovaTech products.

## How to make a warranty claim

Warranty claims are handled by the brand's authorised service centre. Customers can contact the brand directly, or ask NovaMart Support or any NovaMart store to register the claim for them.

The invoice is required for every claim. NovaMart invoices are available on the order page.

## What the warranty does not cover

The manufacturer's warranty does not cover:

- Physical damage, liquid damage or damage from power surges.
- Repairs or modifications by anyone other than an authorised service centre.
- Normal wear of consumable parts, such as remote batteries and filters.
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect how the product works.

## Products that fail within 7 days

A product that does not work when it is first used, or fails within 7 days of delivery, is handled as a defective return, not as a warranty claim. See the Returns and Refunds Policy and the Opened Electronics Policy.

## NovaCare extended warranty

NovaCare extends the manufacturer's warranty by 1 or 2 years. It can be bought with the product or within 30 days of delivery. It is available for laptops, TVs, mobile phones and large appliances.

Platinum members get a free 1-year NovaCare extension on large appliances: refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners.
